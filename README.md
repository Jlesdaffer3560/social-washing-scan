# Green & Social Claims Scan - Hostable v36

Professional hostable version of the Green & Social Claims Risk Assessment tool.

## What is included

- Green and social claims assessment for company websites and claim-related documents.
- Distinction between client-facing / consumer-facing communication and investor, stakeholder or internal documents.
- Three scores: global green + social score, green risk score and social risk score.
- Clearer score interpretation: direct EmpCo and EU Forced Labour Regulation signals are weighted more strongly than broader responsible-business expectations.
- Related-domain checking: when a national website is entered, the scanner cautiously tests a small number of likely related company domains, such as .com or .eu corporate sites.
- External public-source signals exclude company-owned documents, policies, reports and websites; these remain reviewed documents/evidence sources, not external signals.
- More specific recommended actions based on detected claim types.
- Downloadable two-page PDF report, claim register CSV export and detailed methodology PDF.
- Disclaimer moved to the end of the web page and the PDF report.
- “Red Flags” section now includes explicit potential EmpCo and Forced Labour Regulation red flags where relevant.

## Main regulatory lenses

- Green claims: Directive (EU) 2024/825 / EmpCo logic for consumer-facing environmental claims.
- Social/product/supplier claims: Regulation (EU) 2024/3015 / EU Forced Labour Regulation lens for product, supplier, import/export, traceability, modern slavery and forced-labour claims.
- Broader responsible-business expectations: OECD Guidelines, UN Guiding Principles, UN Global Compact and CSRD/ESRS are used as supporting reference frameworks.

## Core scoring model

Each green and social score uses the same high-level model:

- 30% claim wording risk
- 30% substantiation / evidence-gap risk
- 25% external contradictory-context risk
- 15% sector exposure

Direct risk signals under EmpCo or the EU Forced Labour Regulation receive higher interpretive weight than broader OECD/UNGC/UNGP-style claims. Sector exposure is a context factor and should not create a High-risk result on its own.

## Disclaimer

This tool provides an indicative first-pass assessment only. It is not legal advice, does not establish a legal violation and does not make a definitive greenwashing or social-washing finding. Results should be verified by legal, compliance and subject-matter experts before external use.
