# v61 - Logical two-page company report structure

## What changed

- Page 2 now follows a clear sequence: additional claim findings, external public-source context, priority actions, and closing methodology/source notes.
- External public-source signals are now a dedicated full-width report section and are no longer placed beside methodology text.
- Up to two retained external signals are displayed in separate context cards.
- A caution note clarifies that external signals require manual verification of status, entity link and claim relevance.
- Priority actions now follow the external-context section, so recommendations are presented after both internal findings and public-source context.
- Sources reviewed and the methodology reference remain in a separate compact closing panel.
- Repeated claim categories are deduplicated in the executive summary.
- The output remains fixed at exactly two A4 pages.
