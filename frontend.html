Purpose of this scan

This tool reviews company websites and claim-related documents for greenwashing and social-washing risk signals. It is not a broad ESG rating. It helps identify claims that may be too broad, insufficiently evidenced, unsuitable for the intended audience, or potentially contradicted by public-source context.

1. Claim wording
Are there broad, generic or absolute green/social claims?
2. Evidence gap
Are scope, methodology, KPIs, limits, verification, grievance/remedy, audit coverage, product traceability or forced-labour due-diligence evidence missing?
3. External context
Are there relevant public signals that may contradict the claim?
4. Audience, sector & regulation
Is the material consumer-facing, or mainly investor/internal reporting? Are product/supplier claims exposed to forced-labour regulation expectations?

How to interpret the result

How to interpret the result: direct risk signals under EmpCo / Directive (EU) 2024/825 and the EU Forced Labour Regulation / Regulation (EU) 2024/3015 carry more weight than broader responsible-business claims mainly linked to OECD Guidelines, UNGC or UNGP expectations. A high score should normally reflect a sensitive claim, a substantiation gap and/or relevant external contradiction. Consumer-facing green claims receive stricter EmpCo relevance than investor or sustainability-report language used mainly as evidence.

Some corporate websites block automated crawlers. If that happens, the scan will show an error message. Try the national website, the sustainability page, or a specific green, social, supplier, product, sustainability or human-rights page instead of the homepage.
