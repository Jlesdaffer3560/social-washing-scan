Purpose of this scan

This is a claims-risk scan, not a broad ESG rating. It identifies wording that may be too broad, insufficiently evidenced, consumer-facing under EmpCo, or potentially contradicted by relevant external context.

  1. Claim wording: broad, generic, absolute, future-oriented, comparative or label/icon-based claims.
  2. Evidence gap: missing scope, methodology, KPIs, limits, verification, grievance/remedy, audit coverage, traceability or due-diligence evidence.
  3. External context: relevant public signals that may contradict or qualify the claim.
  4. Regulatory lens: EmpCo / Directive (EU) 2024/825 for consumer-facing green claims and Regulation (EU) 2024/3015 for forced-labour product or supply-chain claims.
