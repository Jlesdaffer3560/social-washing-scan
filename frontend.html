Purpose of this scan

This tool reviews a company website, folder or report-style page for greenwashing and social-washing risk signals. It is not a broad ESG score and it does not conclude that greenwashing or social washing has occurred. It helps users identify claims that may be too broad, insufficiently evidenced or potentially contradicted by public-source context.

1. Claim wording
Are there broad, generic or absolute green/social claims?
2. Evidence gap
Are scope, methodology, KPIs, limits, verification, grievance/remedy, audit coverage, product traceability or forced-labour due-diligence evidence missing?
3. External context
Are there relevant public signals that may contradict the claim?
4. Audience, sector & regulation
Is the material consumer-facing, or mainly investor/stakeholder reporting? Are product/supplier claims exposed to forced-labour regulation expectations?

How to interpret the result

High risk should only appear where a sensitive/broad claim is combined with a clear substantiation gap and relevant contradictory context. The scan distinguishes consumer-facing material, where EmpCo relevance is highest, from annual/sustainability reports used mainly as evidence sources. For social claims, the forced-labour lens is especially relevant where wording refers to products, suppliers, responsible sourcing, imports/exports, traceability, modern slavery or forced-labour-free supply chains.

Some corporate websites block automated crawlers. If that happens, the scan will show an error message. Try the national website, the sustainability page, or a specific green, social, supplier, product, sustainability or human-rights page instead of the homepage.
