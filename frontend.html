Purpose of this scan

This tool reviews a company website for social-washing risk signals. It is not a broad ESG score and it does not conclude that social washing has occurred. It helps users identify claims that may be too broad, insufficiently evidenced or potentially contradicted by public-source context.

1. Claim wording
Are there broad or absolute social claims?
2. Evidence gap
Are scope, KPIs, limits, grievance/remedy or audit coverage missing?
3. External context
Are there relevant public signals that may contradict the claim?
4. Sector sensitivity
Does the sector increase the evidence burden?

How to interpret the result

High risk should only appear where a sensitive/broad claim is combined with a clear substantiation gap and relevant contradictory context. Sector risk alone should not drive a high score.

Some corporate websites block automated crawlers. If that happens, the scan will show an error message. Try the national website, the sustainability page, or a specific social/supplier/human-rights page instead of the homepage.
