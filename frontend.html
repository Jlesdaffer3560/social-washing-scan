Sustainability Scan

Assess whether public sustainability claims are specific, substantiated, audience-appropriate and consistent with relevant public information. The scan applies four lenses: EmpCo (Directive (EU) 2024/825), the EU Green Claims Directive (2024), the EU Forced Labour Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/3015), and the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) for deforestation-sensitive commodity claims.

Purpose of this scan

This is a claims-risk scan, not a broad ESG rating. It identifies sustainability wording that may be too broad, insufficiently evidenced, consumer-facing under EmpCo, exposed to the EU Forced Labour Regulation, or potentially contradicted by relevant external public-source context.

  1. Claim wording: broad, generic, absolute, future-oriented, comparative or label/icon-based claims.
  2. Evidence gap: missing scope, methodology, KPIs, limits, verification, grievance/remedy, audit coverage, traceability or due-diligence evidence.
  3. External context: relevant public signals that may contradict or qualify the claim.
  4. Regulatory lens: EmpCo and the EU Green Claims Directive for consumer-facing green claims; Regulation (EU) 2024/3015 for forced-labour claims; EUDR for deforestation-sensitive commodity claims. CSDDD and CSRD thresholds updated for Omnibus I (Directive 2026/470, in force 18 March 2026).
