Sustainability Scan

Assess whether public sustainability claims are specific, substantiated, audience-appropriate and consistent with relevant public information. The scan applies two regulatory lenses: EmpCo — Directive (EU) 2024/825, officially the "Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition" Directive — for consumer-facing environmental (green) claims, and the EU Forced Labour Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/3015) for product, supplier and supply-chain claims related to forced labour.

Assess sustainability claims for wording risk, evidence gaps, regulatory sensitivity and relevant public contradictions — not a broad ESG rating.

How the assessment works

This is a claims-risk scan, not a broad ESG rating. It identifies sustainability wording that may be too broad, insufficiently evidenced, consumer-facing under EmpCo, may create forced-labour-related assurance risk, or may be contradicted by relevant external public-source context.

  1. Claim wording: broad, generic, absolute, future-oriented, comparative or label/icon-based claims.
  2. Evidence gap: missing scope, methodology, KPIs, limits, verification, grievance/remedy, audit coverage, traceability or due-diligence evidence.
  3. External context: relevant public signals that may contradict or qualify the claim.
  4. Regulatory lens: EmpCo (Directive (EU) 2024/825, "Empowering Consumers for the Green Transition") for consumer-facing green claims; the EU Forced Labour Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/3015) for forced-labour and supply-chain claims.
