Climate-neutrality or offsetting claim High

Puratos marks major sustainability milestone with carbon neutral operations.
Why it matters
The wording may imply complete neutrality without making the emissions boundary, residual emissions and role of offsets clear.
Evidence gap
Scope, baseline, methodology, residual emissions, offset use and reporting period.
Recommended wording Separate verified emissions reductions from any offsetting and disclose scopes, baseline, methodology, residual emissions, offset use and progress.

Sustainability label / certification claim High
Source: Puratos 2025 Sustainability GRI Report.pdf
Cacao-Trace, our own certified sustainable cocoa sourcing program.
Why it matters
The wording may be understood as an independent sustainability certification.
Evidence gap
Scheme owner, criteria, independent verifier, audit basis, scope and validity period.
Recommended wording Name the scheme owner, criteria, independent verification basis, audited scope and validity period, or clarify that it is a company programme rather than an independent certification.
Generic environmental claim High
Source: https://www.puratos.com
We support you in creating nutritious and more sustainable breads.
Why it matters
The comparative benefit and environmental attribute are not clear from the wording alone.
Evidence gap
Specific attribute, comparison baseline, method, product scope, period and limitations.
Recommended wording Specify the exact environmental attribute, product scope, comparison baseline, method, evidence, reporting period and limitations.
